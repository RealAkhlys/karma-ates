🔬 Scientific Guide: Understanding Combustion in Freefall

Fire on Earth is so familiar that we mistake buoyancy-driven artifacts for fundamental combustion physics. When gravitational acceleration is eliminated during freefall, fire reveals its pure, diffusion-governed molecular behavior.

1

The Death of Buoyancy

On Earth, buoyant force density is $F_b = (\rho_\infty - \rho_f) g$. Because combustion gases reach 1800K, their density drops by ~80%, rocketing upward at 2-3 m/s. In freefall ($g=0$), buoyant force vanishes identically. There is no "up" or "down" for the fire.

2

Pure Molecular Diffusion

Without convection, mass transfer operates purely via Fick's Second Law: $\frac{\partial C}{\partial t} = D \nabla^2 C$. Because diffusion is isotropic (equal in all radial directions), flames naturally organize into perfect spheres.

3

The Cool Flame Paradox

First verified in the NASA FLEX project on the ISS, large hydrocarbon droplets whose visible flame extinguished continued burning without visible light or soot at 200°C–400°C. This low-temperature oxidation is a critical hidden risk for deep-space habitats.

Governing Dimensionless Groups in Spacecraft Fire Safety

Richardson Number ($Ri$)
$$Ri = \frac{Gr}{Re^2} = \frac{g \beta (T_f - T_\infty) L}{v^2}$$

Ratios natural buoyancy to forced ventilation. On Earth, $Ri \gg 1$ (buoyancy dominates). In ISS ventilation ducts ($v \sim 0.1\text{ m/s}, g \sim 0$), $Ri \approx 0$, proving forced airflow governs flame spread.

Damköhler Number ($Da$)
$$Da = \frac{\tau_{\text{flow}}}{\tau_{\text{chem}}} = \frac{L / v}{1 / (A [O_2]^n e^{-E_a/RT})}$$

Ratios flow residence time to chemical reaction time. If ventilation draft is too fast ($Da < Da_{\text{crit}}$), convective heat loss exceeds chemical heat generation, causing blowoff extinction.
